# Privacy and Public-Release Policy

The publication snapshot is intended to expose the code and non-identifying evidence needed to understand and reproduce the reported analysis while minimizing disclosure of participant-identifying information.

- Use pseudonymous identifiers such as `subject_001` and `subject_002`.
- Do not publish participant names or relationship information.
- Do not publish identifiable face screenshots unless explicit publication consent has been documented and their inclusion is necessary.
- Do not publish unnecessary private health, sleep, activity, or contextual information.
- Keep the validated numerical result files unchanged.
- Redactions made only for privacy should be clearly marked in any public narrative copy.

The private research repository remains the archival record for internal, unredacted experiment notes.
